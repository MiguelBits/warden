# Family impersonation

The sender pretends to be a child or a parent. Phrases include ola mae, ola pai, hi mom, mudei de numero, new number, and sou eu. They ask for an MB WAY code, a Multibanco code, or a transfer, and they say agora, ja, or now. They add nao digas, dont tell, or segredo so the person will not call the real relative.
