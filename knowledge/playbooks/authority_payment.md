# Authority payment demand

A caller claims to be the police, the tax office, or another public authority. The message uses policia, financas, or seguranca social. It demands an immediate transfer, names an IBAN, and asks the person to read a codigo. Pressure lines include transfira, ja, and nao digas a ninguem. The stated threat is detencao or a frozen account.

A real agency does not collect a transfer or a one-time code over the phone.
