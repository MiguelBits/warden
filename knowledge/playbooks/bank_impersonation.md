# Bank impersonation

The sender claims to be the bank. Wording includes bank, banco, card, blocked, suspicious, and payment. They ask the person to read a one-time code, an SMS code, or a codigo, and they say now or immediately. Some add do not tell anyone.

A bank can send an alert. It does not ask the customer to read that code back.
