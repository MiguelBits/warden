# Delivery phishing

The message pretends to be a carrier. Wording includes ctt, encomenda, package, parcel, customs, fee, and taxa. It says the parcel is held or retida, tells the person to pague or pay, and includes an http link. Urgency shows up as agora, urgent, or now.

The carrier's own application is the place to check a parcel. A payment link in the SMS is the hook.
